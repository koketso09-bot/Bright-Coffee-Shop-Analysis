# Bright-Coffee-Shop-Analysis

# Project Overview

This project analyses Bright Coffee Shop's transactional sales data to identify revenue-generating products, peak sales periods, and opportunities to improve business performance using data analytics and visualization.

# Stage 1: Planning & Architecture (CANVAS)

Designed a data flow diagram illustrating how data moves from the source through processing, storage, analysis, and reporting. Identified key business questions, required calculations, and expected insights.

# Stage 2: Data Processing (Databricks)

Converted the source dataset into CSV format and loaded it into Databricks for processing. Cleaned data types, created transaction time buckets, and calculated total sales revenue using unit price and transaction quantity.

# Stage 3: Data Analysis (Excel)

Used EXCEL to analyse revenue, product performance, quantities sold, and sales trends across time intervals and developed pivot tables and charts to highlight top-performing products and peak sales periods.

# Stage 4: Presentation & Recommendations (PowerPoint & Dashboards)

Presented key findings through visualizations and developed recommendations to improve sales performance. Proposed targeted marketing, better inventory planning, promotion of underperforming products, and automated sales reporting.

Key Insights (Jan – Jun 2023)

# Overall Revenue Performance
Bright Coffee Shop generated a total revenue of $698,812.33 between January and June 2023. The three store locations performed almost identically in terms of revenue share: Hell's Kitchen (33.84%), Astoria (33.23%), and Lower Manhattan (32.92%).

# Time-of-Day Sales Patterns

· Peak Morning is the busiest period, accounting for 32% of total sales across all stores.
· Morning follows at 24% of sales.
· Peak Afternoon contributes 24%.
· Afternoon and Evening together account for roughly 21% and represent the least busy hours.

# Product Category Performance

· Coffee is the favourite category at 39% of revenue.
· Tea is second at 28%.
· Combined, Coffee and Tea make up 67% of total revenue.
· Bakery accounts for 12%, while the remaining 21% is spread across 26 other products.

# Top-Selling Individual Products

· Gourmet brewed coffee (Coffee category) – 25,973 units (23%)
· Brewed Chai tea (Tea category) – 26,250 units (23%)
· Barista Espresso – 24,943 units (22%)
· Brewed Black tea – 17,462 units (16%)
· Hot chocolate – 17,457 units (16%)

# Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategic recommendations are proposed:

1. Product Customization: Allow customers to customize milk, toppings, flavours, and coffee strength to increase average transaction value and customer satisfaction.
2. Brand Differentiation: Use social media, promotions, and consistent branding to differentiate Bright Coffee Shop from competitors.
3. Digital Ordering & Drive-Thru: Introduce digital ordering and a drive-thru system on mobile locations to reduce waiting times, especially during the high-traffic Peak Morning period.
4. Loyalty Program: Create a membership program and reward customers with loyalty points to drive repeat business and increase Monday-to-Friday sales.
5. Seasonal Offerings: Introduce summer drinks and ice creams to boost sales during hot seasons, addressing the January/February sales dip.
6. Franchise Expansion: Expand the franchise to new locations, particularly in Queens, since Astoria is currently the only shop serving that area.
